          How it decides

          The model reads the subject and message, turns the words and two-word phrases into numbers with TF-IDF, and a logistic regression model converts those numbers into a spam probability. A probability of 50% or more is called spam.

          Separately, simple rules look for warning signs such as shortened links, shouting, pressure phrases and requests to verify an account. These are shown for context and do not change the score.
